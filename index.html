For Authors

Submission & Manuscript Preparation

How to Submit

Submit a DOCX manuscript and supporting files to naturenoodle.editor@yahoo.com.

Minimum Requirements

Abstract of at least 100 words; 3-6 keywords; main text of at least 250 words, excluding references, tables, and captions.

Editorial Review

All submissions receive editorial assessment. Suitable manuscripts may be evaluated by independent reviewers. The Editor-in-Chief makes the final decision.

Required Statements

Include funding, competing-interest, data-availability, ethics, and author-contribution statements where applicable.

Author Fees: $0

No submission fee, article processing charge, publication fee, page charge, color-figure charge, or reader-access fee.

Access & Licensing

Open Access Policy

All content is immediately available without subscription, registration, payment, or embargo. Authors retain copyright and articles are distributed under CC BY 4.0.
