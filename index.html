About the Journal

Aims & Scope

Nature Noodle publishes carefully documented practical, scientific, cultural, and interdisciplinary work on noodles and related food subjects.

Core Subjects

Noodle ingredients and processing; food chemistry and texture; culinary methods; sensory evaluation; nutrition; sustainability; food history, culture, health, and communication.

Article Types

Research articles, reviews, culinary methods reports, exploratory kitchen studies, short communications, and perspectives.

Languages

English, Chinese, and bilingual Chinese-English manuscripts are accepted.

For Authors

Submission & Manuscript Preparation

How to Submit

Submit a DOCX manuscript and supporting files to naturenoodle.editor@yahoo.com.

Subject: Nature Noodle Submission - Corresponding Author - Short Title

Figures: JPG or PNG. Tables: editable format.

Minimum Requirements

  • Abstract of at least 100 words
  • Three to six keywords
  • Main text of at least 250 words, excluding abstract, references, tables, and captions
  • Author and corresponding-author information
  • Clearly labeled sections and captioned figures/tables

Required Statements

Include funding, competing-interest, data-availability, ethics, author-contribution, and acknowledgment statements where applicable.

Editorial Review

All submissions receive an initial editorial assessment. Suitable manuscripts may be evaluated by one or more independent reviewers. The Editor-in-Chief makes the final editorial decision.

Author Fees: $0

Nature Noodle charges no submission fee, article processing charge, publication fee, page charge, color-figure charge, or reader-access fee.

Editorial Standards

Publication Policies

Originality & Attribution

Submissions must be original, acknowledge sources, and not be under consideration elsewhere.

Conflicts of Interest

Authors, editors, and reviewers should disclose relationships that could reasonably influence the work or editorial decision.

Data & Reproducibility

Methods should support critical evaluation, and data-availability information should be supplied where applicable.

Corrections & Retractions

Material errors may be corrected. Content may be retracted when findings are unreliable or publication ethics have been seriously breached.
