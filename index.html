For Authors

How to Submit

Nature Noodle accepts manuscripts in Chinese, English, or bilingual Chinese-English format. Suitable manuscript types include research articles, reviews, culinary methods reports, exploratory kitchen studies, short communications, and perspectives.

Submission

Email the manuscript and supporting files to naturenoodle.editor@yahoo.com.

Subject line: Nature Noodle Submission - Author Name - Short Title

Manuscript: DOCX · Figures: JPG or PNG · Tables: editable format

Basic Requirements

Include a title, author information, corresponding-author email, abstract, 3-6 keywords, clearly labeled sections, figures and tables with captions, references when applicable, and funding, competing-interest, data-availability, and ethics statements when applicable.

Editorial Review

All submissions receive an initial editorial assessment. Suitable manuscripts may be evaluated by independent reviewers with relevant subject knowledge.

Originality

Submissions must be original, appropriately credit all sources, and must not be simultaneously under consideration by another publication.

Author fees: $0. No submission fee, article processing charge, publication fee, page charge, color-figure charge, or reader-access fee.
