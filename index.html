About the Journal

Aims & Scope

Nature Noodle provides an open-access venue for carefully documented work on noodles and related food subjects. The journal welcomes practical, scientific, cultural, and interdisciplinary approaches, provided that methods, evidence, limitations, and sources are reported transparently.

Core Subjects

  • Noodle ingredients and processing
  • Food chemistry and texture
  • Culinary methods and sensory evaluation
  • Nutrition and food sustainability
  • Food history, culture, and communication

Article Types

  • Research Article
  • Review Article
  • Culinary Methods Report
  • Exploratory Kitchen Study
  • Short Communication
  • Perspective

Languages

Manuscripts may be submitted in English, Chinese, or bilingual Chinese-English format.

The journal encourages clear language and accurate translation of titles, abstracts, keywords, captions, and core findings.

For Authors

Submission & Manuscript Preparation

Nature Noodle accepts original manuscripts that fall within the journal’s aims and scope. Read the requirements below before submitting.

How to Submit

  1. Prepare the manuscript in DOCX format and keep tables editable.
  2. Provide figures as separate JPG or PNG files when possible.
  3. Email the manuscript and supporting files to naturenoodle.editor@yahoo.com.
  4. Use the subject line: Nature Noodle Submission – Corresponding Author – Short Title.

Minimum Requirements

  • Article title
  • Author names and affiliations or professional identities
  • Corresponding-author email address
  • Abstract of at least 100 words
  • Three to six keywords
  • Main text of at least 250 words, excluding the abstract, references, tables, and figure captions
  • Clearly labeled sections appropriate to the manuscript type
  • Figures and tables with descriptive captions

Required Statements

Include the following where applicable:

  • Funding statement
  • Competing-interest declaration
  • Data-availability statement
  • Ethics statement
  • Author-contribution statement
  • Acknowledgments

References must accurately identify the sources used.

Editorial Review

All submissions undergo an initial editorial assessment for scope, completeness, clarity, originality, and reporting quality.

Manuscripts that pass the initial assessment may be sent to one or more independent reviewers with relevant subject expertise. The Editor-in-Chief makes the final editorial decision.

Editorial Standards

Publication Policies

Originality & Attribution

Submissions must be original, appropriately acknowledge all sources, and must not be under consideration by another publication at the time of submission.

Conflicts of Interest

Authors, editors, and reviewers should disclose financial, professional, personal, or other relationships that could reasonably be perceived to influence the work or editorial decision.

Data & Reproducibility

Authors should retain underlying data and describe methods with enough detail to support critical evaluation. Data-availability information should be provided when applicable.

Corrections & Retractions

Material errors may be addressed through a correction notice. Content may be retracted when findings are unreliable, publication ethics have been seriously breached, or continued publication would mislead readers.

Human & Animal Research

Research involving human participants or animals must follow applicable ethical and legal requirements. Relevant approval and consent information should be reported when applicable.

Editorial Independence

Editorial decisions should be based on scholarly and evidentiary merit and should not be determined by author fees, commercial relationships, or the author’s nationality, identity, institutional status, or viewpoint.
