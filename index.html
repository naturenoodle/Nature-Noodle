Aims and scope

A focused venue for noodle and food scholarship.

Nature Noodle serves researchers, educators, practitioners, and readers interested in the scientific, technical, nutritional, environmental, historical, and cultural dimensions of noodles and related foods.

The journal considers original research articles, review articles, methods papers, short research communications, and scholarly perspectives. Previously published, redundant, or simultaneously submitted manuscripts are not considered.

For authors

Submission requirements

Submissions must fit the journal's scope, present a clear scholarly contribution, and be written in clear English. Submit an editable manuscript with figures, tables, supplementary files, and required declarations.

Manuscript file

Include the title, article type, abstract, three to six keywords, author names and affiliations, corresponding-author email, main text, figures, tables, captions, references, funding statement, data-availability statement, ethics statement, and competing-interest statement.

No author processing fees

Nature Noodle charges no submission fee, article processing charge, publication fee, or mandatory page or color charge. Editorial decisions are independent of an author's financial circumstances.

Submit by email

Editorial process

Fair, documented, and merit-based review.

1

Initial assessment

Scope, completeness, declarations, originality concerns, and scholarly suitability are checked.

2

Independent review

Suitable research and review manuscripts are evaluated by at least two subject-qualified reviewers.

3

Editorial decision

The editor considers the manuscript, reports, responses, and ethics requirements.

4

Publication record

Accepted work receives a date, designation, citation, license notice, and stable webpage.

Appeals and complaints: Send reasoned appeals, complaints, or ethics concerns to the editorial email. Decisions and corrective actions are documented.

Journal policies

Transparent publishing standards

Open Access Policy

Nature Noodle provides immediate, worldwide, free access to every published article. No subscription, payment, registration, or embargo is required.

Authors retain copyright and grant Nature Noodle the nonexclusive right of first publication. Articles are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0), permitting reuse with credit, a license link, and identification of changes.

There are no reader access fees, submission fees, article processing charges, or publication fees.

Originality and authorship

Submissions must be original, not under consideration elsewhere, and appropriately credit all sources and contributors. Every listed author must approve the manuscript and accept responsibility for it.

Research and publication ethics

Authors must disclose funding and competing interests and obtain applicable approvals. Fabrication, falsification, plagiarism, duplicate publication, manipulated evidence, and inappropriate authorship are prohibited.

Copyright and licensing

Authors retain copyright and grant Nature Noodle the nonexclusive right of first publication. Articles are published under CC BY 4.0.

Corrections and retractions

Material errors are addressed through dated corrections linked to the original article. Retractions or expressions of concern may be issued when reliability or publication integrity is substantially affected.

Data and reproducibility

Authors should make supporting data and methods available when legally and ethically possible and explain applicable restrictions.

Use of generative AI

AI tools may not be authors. Authors remain responsible for accuracy, originality, citations, confidentiality, and disclosure of substantive AI use.
